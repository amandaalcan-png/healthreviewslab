Timeline Variability Is the Main Complaint

Nerve discomfort has multiple potential underlying causes (oxidative stress, poor circulation, nutrient deficiency, nerve compression), and the formula's ingredients work gradually rather than providing immediate relief. Some users report noticing changes within 2-3 weeks, while others need 60+ days — this variability is consistent with the range of underlying causes nerve discomfort can have, not a formula inconsistency.

Not a Substitute for Diagnosed Condition Management

A secondary complaint pattern comes from users expecting the supplement to resolve nerve pain from diagnosed conditions like severe diabetic neuropathy or nerve compression injuries, which typically require direct medical management alongside any supplement, not instead of it.
