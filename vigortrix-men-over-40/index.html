Vigortrix for Men Over 40

Aromatase activity increases gradually with age. Here's whether the 40s is when this specific mechanism starts to become relevant.

Health Reviews Labs Verdict

Aromatase activity begins increasing gradually from the 30s onward, alongside typical body composition changes, making the 40s a reasonable point where addressing estrogen conversion (alongside testosterone production) starts to become more relevant than it would be for men in their 20s.

When Aromatase Activity Starts Mattering

Body fat percentage tends to increase gradually starting in the 30s for many men, and since aromatase is concentrated in fat tissue, this is when conversion activity typically begins its gradual rise — making the 40s a reasonable transition point where this mechanism becomes more relevant than in younger decades.

Realistic Expectations at This Age

Men in their 40s are likely to see more benefit from a dual approach (testosterone support plus aromatase consideration) than younger men, though given Chrysin's bioavailability limitations, expectations should remain measured rather than dramatic.

What Else Matters at This Age

Body composition management (reducing excess fat tissue through diet and exercise) directly reduces aromatase activity at its source, complementing any supplement approach — this remains one of the more impactful levers available, arguably more so than supplementation alone.
