Health Reviews Labs Verdict

Declining estrogen during menopause directly affects the vaginal and urinary microbiome, often reducing protective Lactobacillus populations and increasing susceptibility to urgency and discomfort. Femicore's Lactobacillus-focused formula addresses this specific microbiome shift, making menopause a particularly relevant context for this type of formula.

How Menopause Changes the Urinary Microbiome

Estrogen supports the growth of protective Lactobacillus species in the vaginal and urinary tract. As estrogen declines during menopause, these protective bacterial populations often decrease, allowing less favorable bacteria to become more dominant — a well-documented mechanism behind increased urinary symptoms during this transition.

Why Probiotic Support Is Particularly Relevant Here

Since the menopausal urinary symptom pattern is specifically linked to Lactobacillus decline, directly supplementing these same bacterial strains (as Femicore does) is a mechanistically well-matched approach for this population, more so than for younger women whose urinary symptoms may have different underlying causes.

What Else Helps During This Transition

Vaginal moisturizers, pelvic floor exercises, and in some cases, discussing topical estrogen therapy with a doctor, all address complementary aspects of menopausal urinary health alongside probiotic support.
