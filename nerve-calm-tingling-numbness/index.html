Tingling and numbness (paresthesia) typically results from nerve irritation, compression, or damage affecting normal signal transmission. Nerve Calm's combination of antioxidant protection (ALA), nerve regeneration support (Benfotiamine), and calming botanicals addresses several contributing factors, though sudden or severe onset warrants medical evaluation first.

What Causes Tingling and Numbness

Paresthesia can result from temporary nerve compression (like sitting cross-legged too long), chronic conditions like diabetic neuropathy, vitamin deficiencies (particularly B12), or general age-related nerve irritation from oxidative stress and reduced circulation. Identifying the likely cause matters for knowing what kind of intervention is appropriate.

How the Formula Addresses Common Causes

For age-related or circulation-related tingling, ALA's antioxidant protection and the formula's circulation-supporting botanicals address plausible contributing mechanisms. For tingling from vitamin deficiency, this formula's B-vitamin complex may help, though a targeted supplement would address a confirmed deficiency more directly.

When to See a Doctor First

Sudden onset, one-sided symptoms, or numbness accompanied by weakness or loss of coordination warrant prompt medical evaluation to rule out more serious causes like nerve compression injuries or circulation problems requiring direct treatment, before trying any supplement.
