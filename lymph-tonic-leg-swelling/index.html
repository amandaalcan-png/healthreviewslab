Health Reviews Labs Verdict

For mild, lifestyle-related leg swelling (from prolonged sitting or standing), Lymph Tonic's Horse Chestnut component has genuine Cochrane-reviewed research support for reduced swelling and heaviness. For swelling caused by underlying medical conditions (heart, kidney, or venous disease), this is not an appropriate substitute for medical treatment.

What Kind of Leg Swelling This Addresses

Mild, symmetrical (both legs) swelling that builds throughout the day and improves overnight — typically related to prolonged sitting, standing, or reduced circulation with age — is the pattern Lymph Tonic's ingredients are researched for. This is different from sudden, one-sided, or painful swelling, which warrants medical evaluation regardless of any supplement.

The Research Behind Horse Chestnut for Swelling

Horse Chestnut extract, standardized for aescin content, has been reviewed by the Cochrane Collaboration specifically for chronic venous insufficiency and leg swelling, with multiple trials showing meaningful reduction in swelling and the sensation of heaviness — among the better-supported natural ingredients in this category.

Realistic Timeline

Users typically report reduced morning puffiness and ankle swelling within 1-2 weeks, with more noticeable leg heaviness improvement building over 3-4 weeks — consistent with the formula's designed 4-8 week protocol window.
